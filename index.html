About Chawla Classes

Delhi's most trusted coaching institute — where every student's success is our mission.

Founded by Mrs. Manju Chawla, Chawla Classes has been shaping academic futures for over 24 years. With expert faculty, small batch sizes and a deeply personal approach to teaching, we ensure that every child gets the attention they deserve.

From Classes 1st to 12th, Commerce, Arts, B.Com, BBA, B.A. to competitive exams like CUET & SSC — we cover it all under one roof in Uttam Nagar, New Delhi.
