Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about admissions, courses, fees, batches and results.

What is the admission process?

Visit our centre or fill the enquiry form online. Our team will guide you through document submission, batch selection and fee payment.

Is a free demo class available?

Yes, students can attend a free demo class before taking admission so they can experience our teaching style firsthand.

What are the batch timings?

We offer morning and evening batches for every class, with weekend special batches also available.

What is the fee structure?

Fees are affordable and competitive, with special discounts for siblings. Call us for the latest fee details.

How often are tests conducted?

Regular weekly tests and performance assessments are conducted to track every student's progress.

Where is Chawla Classes located?

We're located at Shop 72, Hastsal Road, Opp. Sabji Mandi, Near Metro Pillar 657, Uttam Nagar, New Delhi.
