Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about admissions, courses, fees, batches and results at Chawla Classes.

Admissions

What is the admission process?

Visit our centre in Uttam Nagar or fill the online enquiry form. Our team will guide you through document submission, batch selection and fee payment — admission is usually completed the same day.

Is a demo class available before admission?

Yes, we offer a free demo class so students and parents can experience our teaching style, faculty and classroom environment before enrolling.

Is admission open right now?

Admissions for the 2026-27 session are currently open for Classes 1st–12th, B.Com and BBA. New batches start regularly through the year.

Courses & Batches

Which courses are available?

We teach Classes 1st–12th (all subjects), Commerce, Arts, B.Com and BBA, with dedicated faculty for each stream.

What are the batch timings?

We run both morning and evening batches for every class, with flexible weekend batches also available for working students.

How big are the batches?

We deliberately keep batch sizes small so every student gets personal attention and their doubts are cleared in class itself.

Fees

What is the fee structure?

Our fees are affordable and competitive compared to other institutes in Uttam Nagar. Exact fees vary by class and subject — call us for the latest details.

Do you offer a sibling discount?

Yes, we offer special discounts for siblings enrolled at Chawla Classes. Ask our team when you visit or call.

How can I pay the fees?

Fees can be paid at our centre directly, or you can ask our staff about available online payment options.

Results & Performance

How often are tests conducted?

We conduct regular weekly tests and performance assessments to track every student's progress throughout the year.

Do you share progress reports with parents?

Yes, we keep parents updated on their child's test performance and overall progress on a regular basis.

What results have your students achieved?

Our students consistently achieve 95%+ board results, with 10,000+ students guided successfully over 24+ years.
